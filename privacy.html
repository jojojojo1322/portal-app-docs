Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what the Portal Notices for Jira Service Management app ("the app"), provided by Bobob Apps ("we"), stores and why. The app is built on Atlassian Forge and runs inside your Atlassian cloud.

Summary

  • The app stores only what it needs to show notices and remember who closed them.
  • Everything is stored in Atlassian's Forge storage in your Atlassian cloud. The app sends no data outside Atlassian and uses no analytics or tracking services.
  • We, the developer, cannot read your stored data.

What the app stores

DataWhyWhere
Notice content and settings (title, message, schedule, maintenance window, audience, translations, color)To show the notices you createForge storage
Saved templates (notice text and settings, and the account ID of who saved them)So admins can reuse their own templatesForge storage
Atlassian account ID of the person who created or last changed a notice, with timesTo show who changed whatForge storage
For signed-in people: account ID with the time they first saw or closed a notice (for a notice that repeats and shows once, the first view in the latest repeat)To stop showing closed notices and notices set to show once, and to show admins how many people saw oneForge storage
For visitors who aren't signed in: an approximate count of views per notice, with no identifierTo show admins roughly how many visitors saw a noticeForge storage
For visitors who aren't signed in: which notices they closed or sawSo the same notice doesn't come backOnly in the visitor's own browser (local storage)
App settings (whether project admins can post notices)To apply your choiceForge storage

The app reads, but does not store, the following from Jira Service Management when needed: the customer organizations of the person viewing the portal (only when a notice targets organizations, and kept in memory for at most five minutes), and the lists of portals, request types and organizations (to let admins choose them).

Who can see it

Jira admins can see notices and counts for every portal; project admins for their own portal. Customers only see the notices meant for them.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the notice exists. Deleting a notice deletes its records. If you uninstall the app, Atlassian handles the stored data under its Forge data lifecycle.

Data location and transfers

Data is stored and processed by Atlassian under its own terms and data residency settings for your site. The app makes no external network calls.

Your rights

To ask about data the app stores for your site, contact your Jira admin, or email us at support@bobob.app. Because the data lives in your Atlassian cloud, your admin can delete notices and their records at any time.

Changes

We will update this page if the app starts storing anything new, and change the date above.
